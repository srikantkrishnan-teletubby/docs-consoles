TB Test — What to Submit and How Long It's Valid

What Clipboard accepts as a TB test

Clipboard accepts any of the following as proof of a negative TB test:

  • Skin test (PPD / Mantoux) — 1-step or 2-step, depending on what your requirement asks for
  • Blood test — QuantiFERON-TB Gold (QFT-G) or T-SPOT.TB
  • Chest X-ray
  • Physician's certificate, note, or letter
  • Illinois Childcare Physical Form or Pennsylvania Childcare Physical Form, where those apply

Two additional documents are accepted only for specific requirements:

  • TB Symptoms Questionnaire — accepted on its own, but only for the requirement called "TB Test or TB Symptoms Questionnaire"
  • Annual TB Screening Questionnaire — for the requirement called "Negative TB test or Chest X-Ray & Annual TB Questionnaire". This one cannot stand alone; it must be submitted together with a chest X-ray

The result must be negative. A positive result cannot be approved — see "My TB test came back positive" below.

Which TB requirement do I have

There are several different TB requirements, and the answer to "how long is my test valid" depends on which one you were given. They are not interchangeable.

To find yours: open the Clipboard app, go to My Account > Documents, and read the exact name of the TB requirement listed there.

Common requirement names include:

  • TB Test
  • TB Test - 6 months or less
  • TB Test or TB Symptoms Questionnaire
  • 2-Step PPD Test
  • 2-Step PPD Test or QuantiFERON TB Test, or Chest X-Ray (1 Year or Less)
  • 2 Step PPD or TB Blood Test (QuantiFERON / T-Spot) or CXR (1 yr or less)
  • 2-Step PPD or TB Blood Test (QFT / TSpot) — 3 months or less
  • Negative Skin Test (PPD) or QuantiFERON or CXR (within 90 days)
  • Negative TB test or Chest X-Ray & Annual TB Questionnaire
  • PPD 90d / TB Blood Test (QFT / TSpot) 6mo / CXR 12mo

Note: Some older or facility-specific requirement names may not appear in the validity table below. If your requirement name is not listed, contact support to verify your validity period.

Not every professional needs a TB test. It depends on your role and the facilities you work at.

Which date the clock starts from:

  • Skin test (PPD): the date the test was read. For a 2-step, the date the second test was read.
  • Blood test: the report date. If only a collection date is shown, that date is used. If both appear, the report date is used.
  • Chest X-ray: the date of service or date of exam.
  • TB Symptoms Questionnaire / Annual TB Screening Questionnaire: the physician's signature date.

Two things worth knowing:

T-SPOT is not accepted for "2-Step PPD Test or QuantiFERON TB Test, or Chest X-Ray (1 Year or Less)" or for "Negative Skin Test (PPD) or QuantiFERON or CXR (within 90 days)". Those two requirements accept QuantiFERON only.

A chest X-ray submitted under "Negative TB test or Chest X-Ray & Annual TB Questionnaire" is valid for 1 year, not 5, because that requirement is an annual screening. If your X-ray is older than a year, a newer TB screening is needed.

Some facilities use shorter windows than the table above. If you work at certain facilities the validity period can be shorter regardless of your requirement name. If your test looks valid by this table but is being treated as expired, that is likely why, and support can check your specific facility.

If your requirement name doesn't appear in the table above, or if you're asked for facility-specific paperwork, contact support and they can confirm what applies to you.

What my document must show

Every TB document must include:

  1. Your full name, matching the name on your Clipboard profile and Personal ID
  2. The result, clearly stated as negative
  3. The date(s) — for a skin test, both the date it was placed and the date it was read
  4. Verification from the provider — either the reader's or physician's signature or printed name, or clinic details such as a letterhead, portal header, clinic contact information, or website

Also:

  • The reading must be done by the clinic that administered it, another licensed professional, or a third-party provider. You cannot read or verify your own test, even if you are qualified to. This applies to everyone.
  • Forms downloaded and filled in by hand are fine, as long as they are signed by the examiner and complete.
  • Photos, photocopies, and scans are fine, as long as everything is legible.
  • Electronic and digital reports are fine, as long as they carry the verification details above.
  • Documents from two different clinics can be combined, as long as the information is consistent.
  • Upload as JPEG or PDF.

Timing rules for skin tests (PPD)

These are the rules a PPD has to meet:

  • Reading window: the test must be read 48 to 72 hours after it was placed. Slightly outside that is acceptable if it still falls within 2 to 3 days.
  • 2-step interval: the second test must be placed 7 days to 3 weeks after the first test was administered.
  • Each step needs both its placement date and its reading date shown.
  • Both results must be negative.

A 2-step PPD can be done at two different clinics. That is fine as long as the dates work.

If your clinic told you to come back for the reading at a time outside the normal window, that can still be accepted. Mention it when you submit.

Why TB tests get rejected

These are the most common reasons, in order of how often they happen.

Screenshot from a patient portal or health app. By far the most common. See the next section.

Missing provider or clinic details. No signature, no printed physician name, no letterhead, no clinic contact details. Nothing on the document ties it to the clinic that performed it.

Wrong document type. Something other than a TB test was uploaded — an MMR titer panel and other lab work are frequent mix-ups.

Missing pages. Often the last page carrying the physician's signature, or the page with the actual result.

Duplicate. The document matches one already on file, usually a resubmission of something previously rejected without the problem being fixed.

Read too late. The gap between the placement date and the reading date is more than 72 hours.

Only one step submitted where two are required, or the two tests are not 7 days to 3 weeks apart.

Expired. Outside the validity window for your requirement.

Name doesn't match. The name on the test differs from the name on your Personal ID.

Illegible. Blurry, cropped, or too dark to read.

Can I submit a screenshot from my patient portal?

Not usually, and this is the single most common reason a TB test gets rejected.

Screenshots from apps like MyChart typically show only the date and the result. They leave out the clinic name, the physician, the official header, and the other details needed to verify the document is genuine.

What to do instead: open the same result in the portal and look for a Print or Download button. That generates the full official report, which includes everything needed. Upload that version.

If your portal has no print or download option, or you can't get the full version from your clinic, Clipboard can arrange a new TB test for you at no cost. See the next section.

My TB test came back positive

A positive result cannot be approved on its own, but it does not stop you from working with Clipboard. There are two routes.

Option 1 — chest X-ray plus questionnaire. Get a chest X-ray. If it shows no active TB, submit it along with an Annual TB Screening Questionnaire. Once you have a negative chest X-ray on file it stays valid for 5 years, so in later years you only need to submit a new Annual TB Screening Questionnaire rather than repeating the X-ray.

Option 2 — blood test. Submit a negative QuantiFERON or T-SPOT blood test instead.

If your chest X-ray mentions a history of positive PPD, an Annual TB Screening Questionnaire will be needed alongside it.

Clipboard can provide an authorization form for your clinic for any of these — see below.

I don't have a TB test. Can Clipboard help me get one?

Yes. Clipboard offers a free TB test through our partner Checkr and covers the full cost.

It is a 1-step PPD skin test. You choose a nearby location and book a time that suits you. Once it's done the result comes back to Clipboard automatically and is added to your profile for you.

This is available if:

  • Your TB test was rejected and you're not sure how to get a valid one
  • You can't get the full version of your results from your clinic or portal
  • Cost is a barrier
  • You simply ask for it

Just ask support and an invitation will be sent to your email.

After you get the invitation:

  • You have 7 days to respond and book your appointment
  • Once booked, your test pass is valid for 10 days
  • If you need longer, ask support — a one-time extension can be added
  • To change location, open the Candidate Portal and select Change Location under Health Information

If you already have a pending Checkr invitation, a second one won't be sent. Check your email, including your junk folder.

My clinic wants an authorization form before doing the test

Clipboard can provide one. Ask support and specify which you need:

  • TB Test
  • QuantiFERON or TB blood test
  • Chest X-ray
  • Negative TB test or Chest X-Ray & Annual TB Questionnaire

If your clinic simply needs a form to fill in rather than a formal authorization, any standard TB test form works, as long as it captures your name, the result, the date the test was administered, and the date it was read.

I need a 2-step but only have a 1-step

If you already have an approved 1-step PPD and your requirement calls for a 2-step, you may just need the second step.

The second test has to be placed 7 days to 3 weeks after the first was administered, so timing matters. Contact support and they can arrange the second step for you, including through Checkr.

If you already have two approved PPD results on file, they may be able to be applied to your 2-step requirement without you taking another test. Ask support to check.

The name on my test doesn't match my profile

The name on your TB test needs to match your Clipboard profile and Personal ID. Some variation is allowed — nicknames, a single last name, small typos, and initials for first or middle names are usually fine. Last names need to match.

If your legal name has changed, upload a name change document in the app under My Account > Documents > Workplace Specific > Optional Docs > Name Change Document. Any of these work:

  • Marriage certificate
  • Divorce papers or certificate
  • Birth certificate
  • Legal name change document or court order

Alternatively, upload a current government-issued ID under Personal ID and complete the verification when prompted.

Questions this page does not cover

Route these to a person rather than answering from this page:

  • Whether a specific document will be approved. That decision is made by a document specialist reviewing the actual file.
  • What a test result means for the professional's health, or any medical question. Direct them to their doctor.
  • Requirements for a specific named facility, including any shortened validity window.
  • Any TB requirement name not listed in the validity table above.
  • Disputes about a rejection, or repeated rejections of the same document.
  • A worker who received BCG vaccination and tested positive on a PPD. This case is not yet documented and must go to a specialist.
